Privacy Policy — US Citizen Civic Test 2026

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Summary: Your study progress stays on your device. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only data that can leave your device is audio processed by Apple's speech recognition when you use the optional pronunciation practice feature.

This policy describes how Essential Dev Team ("we," "us") handles information when you use US Citizen Civic Test 2026 (the "App"). We build our apps to collect the minimum data needed to make them work.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Your quiz progress, scores, bookmarks, and settings are stored locally on your device (and in your personal iCloud device backup if you enable backups). We cannot access this data. The App does not require an account, and the civics questions work fully offline.

2. Microphone & Speech Recognition (Pronunciation Practice)

The App offers an optional pronunciation practice feature. If you choose to use it and grant microphone permission:

  • Your speech is processed by Apple's Speech Recognition service, which may transmit the audio to Apple's servers for transcription, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • We never receive, store, or have access to your audio or transcripts — recognition results are used on your device only, to show how your answer was heard.
  • You can decline or revoke microphone and speech permissions at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the App works normally without them.

3. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive anonymized transaction records (for example, that a purchase is active) but never your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No user accounts and no collection of names, emails, or phone numbers
  • No location tracking
  • We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the California CCPA/CPRA

The App may contact Apple's App Store (itunes.apple.com) to check whether a newer version of the App is available. This request contains no personal information.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All data lives on your device: delete the App to remove it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/UK, California, and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be satisfied on-device; for anything else, email us and we will help within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.
